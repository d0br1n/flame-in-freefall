Hand-measure the flame edge

Do this alone, and do not open the pipeline's numbers until you have saved your files. Steps: PROTOCOL.md in this folder.

1. Video

no video Keys: ← → one frame, Shift + arrow 30 frames, Delete removes the mark on this frame.

2. Scale (optional)

The B1, B4 and B13 videos show only the flame and a text overlay on black, so no known length is visible. Skip this step then: positions are saved in pixels, which is the unit the pipeline uses, and the comparison is done in pixels per second. Do not guess a scale. If a video does show a length you know (for example the sample width, 20 mm), click its two ends and type it.

not set

3. Direction

Click the igniter end of the sample (zero), then a second point further along the sample, in the direction the flame spreads.

not set

4. Mark the leading edge

Go to a frame, then click the front of the visible flame sheet, toward the unburned film. About once per second, at least 15 points. If you cannot see the flame, skip that moment.

5. Save
